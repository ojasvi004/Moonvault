# Reach

- **Who:** people who menstruate and want tracking without handing intimate data to an ad-funded app. That matters most where reproductive data can be used against people. Launch focus: urban India (built at ETHGlobal Mumbai): mobile-first, 360 px, Slow-4G friendly, WhatsApp sharing.
- **Doctors and partners** get a clean read-only page from a WhatsApp message or QR code, with no install and no account.
- **Developers:** the `mv.share` pattern (ciphertext in an expiring, owner-revocable entity plus the key in the URL fragment) is a reusable recipe for any "send sensitive data that self-destructs" feature: lab results, IDs, insurance documents.
- **Growth loop:** every share is a doctor seeing the product.
